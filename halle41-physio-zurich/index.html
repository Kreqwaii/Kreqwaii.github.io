Clear Answers for Expats, Runners & Referring Physicians.

With a Swiss GP or specialist prescription (Verordnung), KVG basic insurance covers 9 sessions per series via TarMed direct billing (subject to your annual Franchise and 10% Selbstbehalt). After an accident, UVG covers 100% with zero copay. Without a referral, self-pay physiotherapy is CHF 140 (50 min) or CHF 70 (25 min).
Yes. All 13 physiotherapists in Zurich West and our entire clinical network treat fluently in English and German (plus French, Italian, Spanish, and Russian among specific clinicians). Formal doctor/insurer reports are issued in German while we explain every finding to you in English.
