ACC & Patient Pathway

How Active+ Rehab Works from Day One

Whether you walk straight into an Auckland clinic after a weekend sports sprain or arrive via GP referral for Concussion or Pain Management, the intake pathway stays streamlined.

STEP 01

Lodge ACC45 or Bring Referral

For musculoskeletal and sports injuries, Active+ physios lodge your ACC45 claim directly at your first appointment.

STEP 02

40-60 Min Clinical Assessment

Hands-on orthopaedic testing, biomechanical screening and clear agreement on your co-payment and recovery milestones.

STEP 03

Multidisciplinary Team Input

Seamless escalation to Pilates, occupational therapy, concussion clinicians, pain specialists or dietitians as needed.

STEP 04

Return to Work, Sport & Life

Graduated Stay at Work plans, home exercise progressions and prevention strategies to stop recurring injuries.

A GP, Urgent Care or Hospital referral is only required for specialist ACC contracts such as the Concussion Service (CCS), Pain Management Service (PMS) or Training for Independence (TI).
Active+ holds direct national and regional contracts with ACC for Concussion Services (CCS), Pain Management Services (PMS), Training for Independence (TI), Stay at Work (SAW) and Sensitive Claims (ISSC). Once your ACC claim and referral are approved, ACC funds 100% of the interdisciplinary programme with zero co-payment or hidden fees.
