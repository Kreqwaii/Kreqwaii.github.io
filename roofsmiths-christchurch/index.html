Canterbury Steel Profile & Coating Comparison

Choosing the right gauge, rib geometry, and coating system matters across inland Rolleston frosts, Port Hills wind zones, and salt-laden eastern beaches.

System & Profile Gauge (BMT) Min. Pitch Canterbury Zone Fit Key Architectural Advantage
Colorsteel Endura Corrugate / Styleline 0.40 mm / 0.55 mm 8 deg Inland & Metro NZ's most trusted everyday residential coating for Christchurch, Rolleston, Lincoln & Rangiora.
Colorsteel Maxx (Marine Grade) 0.40 mm / 0.55 mm 3 to 8 deg Severe Coastal Enhanced aluminium-zinc alloy base engineered for Sumner, New Brighton, Lyttelton & Banks Peninsula.
Trapezoidal 5-Rib / ColorCote hi5 0.40 mm / 0.55 mm 3 deg Low-Pitch & Commercial High water-shedding capacity on shallow roofs, villas extensions, and commercial care facilities.
Calder Stewart Heritage Tray / Solar-Rib 0.55 mm 3 deg Architectural High-Wind Concealed fixings with crisp standing seams; can be roll-formed cleanly on site for long clean spans.
Gerard Pressed Metal Tiles 7-Layer Coated Steel 12.5 deg All Wind Zones Up to 50-year guarantee; horizontally fastened against high uplift with textured or satin tile finishes.
Viking Waterproof Membrane Multi-Ply System 1 to 3 deg Flat Roofs & Decks Seamless watertight membrane ideal for flat roofs, internal gutters, parapet aprons, and tanking.
