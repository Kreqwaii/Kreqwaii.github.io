    Financial & Insurance Policy

    ODA Fee Guide, Electronic Claims & Payment

    Fees at Queensway Dental follow the current Ontario Dental Association (ODA) Fee Guide. We file electronic insurance claims via high-speed link directly to your insurer and can submit pre-determinations so you know your exact share ahead of treatment.

    • Accepted payment methods: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Debit, Cash and Cheques.
    • Flexible payment plans are available to keep extensive restorative care manageable.
    • Please allow 2 business days notice for rescheduling or cancellations to avoid a $75 short-notice fee.

    First Visit Preparation

    Arriving at 1024 The Queensway

    Your initial visit includes a relaxed conversation about your dental goals, a full examination of teeth, gums and oral tissues, and low-radiation digital X-rays when clinically indicated.

    • Arrive 10 minutes before your scheduled time to complete your medical and dental history.
    • Bring your dental insurance card or CDCP member letter so our coordinators can verify coverage.
    • Have dental anxiety or past sensitivity? Let our front desk know so we can adjust pacing and breaks.
    When should I bring my child for their first dental check-up +
    Both the Canadian Dental Association and our team recommend scheduling your child's first visit by age 1, or within six months after the first tooth erupts. Early visits build comfort, screen for developmental concerns, and set simple home brushing habits before cavities start.
    Do you remove wisdom teeth in the office without an external referral +
    Yes. For most adolescents and young adults between ages 17 and 21, wisdom teeth can be evaluated with digital panoramic imaging and removed safely right here in our Etobicoke office. Complex cases requiring hospital sedation are coordinated smoothly with trusted local specialists.
    How quickly can you see dental emergencies or broken teeth +
    We set aside dedicated urgent slots every day and stay open until 7:00 pm on Mondays and Thursdays and until 8:00 pm on Tuesdays and Wednesdays. Call 416-251-5334 immediately for severe toothache, swelling, a knocked-out tooth, a lost crown or soft-tissue injury.
    What should I bring if I have employer insurance or CDCP coverage +
    Bring your benefit card (policy/group and certificate/ID numbers) or your CDCP coverage confirmation. Because plan maximums, frequencies and co-payments differ across insurers, we submit electronic pre-determinations whenever requested.
