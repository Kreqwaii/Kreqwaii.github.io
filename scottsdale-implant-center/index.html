Specialized Restorative Scope

Comprehensive Implant & Prosthodontic Procedures

Every restoration is designed for bite balance, bone preservation, and natural facial aesthetics by a board-certified maxillofacial prosthodontist.

Single Dental Implants

Replace a missing tooth from root to crown using a biocompatible titanium post, custom abutment, and natural ceramic crown without altering neighboring healthy teeth.

  • Preserves jawbone density and facial contour
  • Planned with 3D CBCT & YOMI robotic guidance

Full Arch & All-on-4 Implants

Stabilize an entire upper or lower arch of 10 to 14 fixed teeth on four or more titanium implants, eliminating removable denture movement and palate coverage.

  • Immediate provisional teeth placed within 24 hours
  • Engineered for confident chewing and clear speech

Zirconia & Implant Bridges

Non-removable implant bridges and high-strength monolithic zirconia restorations engineered in coordination with our in-house digital workflow and lab.

  • Custom shading matched to natural enamel
  • Options for 3-4 tooth spans or full-mouth arches

Maxillofacial & Full Mouth Reconstruction

Fellowship-trained care for complex surgical reconstruction, facial trauma, head and neck oncology rehabilitation, cleft palate prosthetics, and severe wear.

  • Dual-arch bite alignment and TMJ stability
  • Dedicated collaboration with referring physicians

Technology & Patient Comfort

Robotic Precision, 3D Diagnostics & Sedation

Technology at Scottsdale Center for Implant Dentistry exists to reduce surgical trauma, accelerate biological recovery, and verify restorative accuracy before treatment begins.

01 / ROBOTICS

YOMI Robotic Implant Guidance

The first FDA-cleared robotic dental surgery platform provides real-time haptic and visual tracking for exact implant angle, depth, and position with smaller incisions.

02 / IMAGING

3D Cone Beam CT (CBCT)

Three-dimensional imaging maps bone volume, nerve bundles, and sinus anatomy to fabricate custom surgical guides and eliminate diagnostic guesswork.

03 / SEDATION

IV Sedation With Anesthesiologist

Dr. Bleeker has provided sedation dentistry since 1994, partnering with certified dental anesthesiologists so complex implant appointments feel calm and restful.

04 / DIGITAL LAB

CEREC & Intra-Oral Scanning

Digital optical impressions replace messy putty trays and feed directly into our in-house laboratory and CEREC milling workflow for same-day ceramic restorations.

05 / BIOLOGICS

Fotona Laser & PRF Healing

Minimally invasive Fotona laser tissue contouring paired with autologous Platelet-Rich Fibrin concentrates natural growth factors at implant and grafting sites.

06 / STERILITY

Ozonated Water Protocols

Chemical-free ozonated water irrigates surgical sites and sterilizes clinical surfaces throughout procedures to support clean soft-tissue recovery.
