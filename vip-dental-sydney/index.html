Medibank Members' Choice & Policy Transparency

As a Medibank Members' Choice provider, capped fees apply to specified dental services while rebates depend on your individual Extras cover, annual limits, and waiting periods. Policies such as Gold Ultra Health, Healthy Living Extras, and My Choice Extras 60 offer varying check-up benefits; our reception provides item numbers in advance so you can confirm exact rebates with your fund.

Medicare CDBS: Bulk-Billed up to $1,158 AUD Eligible children aged 0-17 receive up to $1,158 over two calendar years (2026 period, or $1,132 if started in 2025) for exams, X-rays, cleans, sealants, fillings, root canals, and extractions.
STAGED DENTAL PAYMENT OPTIONS

MediPay, TLC, Smile Right, Zip Pay, Afterpay & SuperCare

Accepting major credit cards and EFTPOS alongside flexible external repayment partners so essential preventive, implant, and orthodontic care remains accessible.
