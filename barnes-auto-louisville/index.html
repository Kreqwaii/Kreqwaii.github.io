              Common Questions
            
    
            
    Louisville Driver Questions
    
          
    
        
    

        
    
          
    
            
    Why does the estimator show a USD min–max range instead of one flat figure
    
            
    
              Exact parts cost depends on engine trim, rotor diameter, single-axle versus dual-axle brake wear, or whether an engine fault stems from a sensor or wiring harness. The range prevents lowball guesses while giving you clear budget boundaries.
            
    
          
    

          
    
            
    Why does mileage tier adjust brake and suspension estimates
    
            
    
              Vehicles past 60,000 and 120,000 miles commonly have heat-seized caliper bracket bolts, rusted pinch bolts, or adjacent bushings that require extra extraction labor and replacement hardware.
