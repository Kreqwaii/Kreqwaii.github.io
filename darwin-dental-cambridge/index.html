Clinical Protocol Sequence

Stage 01 · Digital iTero Scan & Occlusal Mapping

All three modalities begin with high-resolution digital scans, clinical photography, and an assessment of enamel thickness, bite forces, and gingival symmetry.

Bioclear Bonding

Additive
Timeline 1 to 2 visits (1 to 2 weeks)
Invasiveness Zero tooth drilling; 100% enamel preserved
Clinical Stages Biofilm blasting, anatomical matrix seating, heated composite injection, mirror polish
Ideal For Black triangles, chipped edges, small gaps, peg laterals

Porcelain Veneers

Ceramic
Timeline 2 to 3 visits (3 to 4 weeks)
Invasiveness Ultra-thin 0.3 to 0.5 mm enamel smoothing when indicated
Clinical Stages Trial smile mock-up, conservative contour, master ceramist fabrication, resin bonding
Ideal For Deep discolouration, major shape redesign, 10 to 15 year gloss stability

Invisalign Aligners

Orthodontic
Timeline 4 to 12 months (7-day tray changes)
Invasiveness Non-restorative alignment of natural teeth
Clinical Stages ClinCheck 3D simulation, discrete tooth-coloured attachments, Vivera night retention
Ideal For Crowding, bite harmony, pre-alignment before whitening or Bioclear
