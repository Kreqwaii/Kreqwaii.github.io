Two Ways We Practice

Tailored Logistics for Small Pets & Large Animals

Choose calm, low-stress mobile care on your property or visit our Salt Lake City veterinary clinic for advanced surgical and imaging support.

In-Clinic Care · Salt Lake City, UT

Ideal for soft-tissue surgery, comprehensive dental extractions, multi-view digital radiographs, and hospitalized diagnostics requiring dedicated monitoring.

  • Zero mobile dispatch fee with scheduled Mon–Sat appointment blocks
  • Sterile surgical suite with continuous pulse-ox, capnography, and thermal support
  • Same-visit in-house hematology, blood chemistry, and diagnostic imaging

Mobile Farm & Home Unit

Designed for anxious companion pets, multi-animal households, equine barns, and backyard herds where transporting patients adds stress or logistical difficulty.

  • Single per-trip call fee covers multiple pets or herd animals at one address
  • Temperature-monitored vaccines, portable ultrasound, dental power float, and field kits
  • Coordinated Mon–Sat route windows across Salt Lake City and neighboring foothills
