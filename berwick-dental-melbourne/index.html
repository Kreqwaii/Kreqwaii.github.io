BERWICK CLINICAL CAPABILITIES

Comprehensive general, cosmetic, and surgical dentistry

From six-monthly preventive cleans with EMS Airflow biofilm therapy to 3D-guided All-on-4 implant rehabilitation and sleep dentistry, every stage is coordinated on High Street.

01 From $3,999 AUD

Single Dental Implants & 3D Surgical Guides

American-made titanium fixtures paired with custom zirconia or porcelain crowns crafted with local Australian laboratories. 3D-printed single-use surgical guides ensure precise angle and depth placement, preserving natural jawbone structure.

  • Complimentary initial implant consultation ($0)
  • Digital Smile Design virtual preview
  • Bone grafting & sinus support where clinically indicated
02 Full-Arch Fixed Solution

All-on-4 Dental Implants

Four strategically positioned implants per arch, with posterior fixtures tilted up to 45 degrees to maximise existing bone density. Patients receive an immediate fixed temporary bridge while osseointegration takes place over six months.

  • Monolithic zirconia, porcelain, or titanium final arch
  • Avoids removable dentures during healing in most cases
  • Fast lab turnarounds & staged payment planning
03 Same-Day Emergency Slots

Wisdom Teeth Removal & Oral Surgery

Diagnostic X-rays evaluate impacted, angled, or decayed third molars before comfortable removal under local anaesthetic, IV sedation, or general anaesthesia. Dissolving sutures eliminate the need for suture-removal visits.

  • Simple & complex surgical wisdom tooth extractions
  • Same-day triage for acute toothache, swelling, or trauma
  • Transparent referral pathway if specialist hospital care is needed
04 AHPRA IV Endorsed

Sleep Dentistry & IV Sedation

Tailored comfort for patients with dental phobia, sensitive gag reflexes, or long surgical appointments. Dr. Evangeline Alphonse provides AHPRA-endorsed IV Sedation alongside Nitrous Oxide, oral sedation, and visiting GA specialists.

  • Continuous breathing, heart rate, and oxygen monitoring
  • Dedicated clinical recovery area before discharge home
  • In-house General Anaesthesia avoids hospital admission

FLEXIBLE REPAYMENTS

Interest-Free Plans & Superannuation Access

Choose an instalment schedule that fits your household budget, or access superannuation early for medically necessary restorative and surgical treatments.

  • Afterpay Split visits into four equal fortnightly payments with 0% interest when paid on time using the Afterpay digital card at reception.
  • Humm (National Dental Plan) Structured interest-free payment plans designed specifically for dental implants, crowns, and orthodontic treatments.
  • TLC (Total Lifestyle Credit) & ZIP / Openpay Weekly, fortnightly, or extended monthly repayment terms covering comprehensive dental and surgical care.
  • In-House Orthodontic Direct Debit Manageable scheduled debits for braces and clear aligner journeys tailored during your free ortho scan.
  • SuperCare (ATO Compassionate Release of Super) No-fee, no-approval facilitation to apply for superannuation funding covering implants, oral surgery, crowns, root canals, and orthodontics.
