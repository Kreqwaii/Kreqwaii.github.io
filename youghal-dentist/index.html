Patient Guidance

Frequently Asked Clinical Questions

Clear answers on check-up intervals, children's first appointments, emergency care, PRSI eligibility and missing tooth options.

The first dental check-up for a child is typically recommended around age 2. The visit is kept short and calm to build positive familiarity with the dental team, review brushing and diet habits, and plan a 6-month follow-up. Under-15 examination plus scale and polish is €60, and protective fissure seals are €35.
Most patients benefit from a check-up every 6 to 12 months depending on gum health, decay risk and medical history. Patients managing gum inflammation, diabetes or frequent tartar buildup may be advised to attend every 3 to 4 months with our hygienist Alma Kelleher.
Under the Department of Social Protection PRSI Treatment Benefit scheme, qualifying contributors are entitled to one free annual oral examination and a subsidised Scale and Polish capped at a €15 patient co-payment. Medical Card (DTSS) entitlements depend on active HSE eligibility and available clinic scheme allocations. Please share your PPS or Medical Card details with Kim, Sinead or Mary at reception so eligibility can be verified ahead of your visit.
When decay or a crack reaches the inner tooth pulp, bacteria cause pain or infection. Emergency appointments (€80) focus on rapid pain relief, X-ray diagnosis and stabilising the tooth. If root canal treatment (endodontics) is required, infected pulp tissue is gently cleared under local anaesthetic to save the natural tooth.
Depending on bone support, neighbouring teeth and budget, your dentist will compare dental implants, fixed porcelain bridges, acrylic partial or full dentures (€500 to €1,000), and precision cobalt-chrome dentures (€1,400).
