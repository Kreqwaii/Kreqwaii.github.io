Every Denplan member at Clifton Dental Care receives worldwide dental emergency cover and injury cover up to £10,000 per incident.

Denplan Essentials & Payment Options

Designed for patients wishing to budget check-ups and hygienist visits while enjoying a 20% discount off standard private treatment fees (excluding whitening and orthodontics).

Level A: Basic Essentials 1 dental check-up, scale and polish with dentist plus 1 x 20-minute hygienist visit per year, with 20% off eligible private treatments.
Level B: Standard Essentials 2 dental check-ups with dentist plus 2 x 20-minute hygienist visits per year, plus 20% off eligible private fees and £10,000 injury insurance.
Level C: Gum Care Essentials 2 dental check-ups with dentist plus 4 x 20-minute hygienist visits per year for targeted periodontal support.
Written Estimate Guarantee In line with Clifton Dental Care Payment Policy, a written estimate and treatment plan is provided after initial consultation before any private or NHS treatment starts. Cash, debit/credit card, Denplan and Interest-Free Credit (subject to status) are welcome.
