    • TMJ jaw pain, tension headaches, and shoulder impingement
    • Chronic tendinitis, whiplash, and groin strain

    Objective high-frequency pressure plate mapping measures exact load transfer and left-right asymmetries during walking and running strides.

    • Custom 3D-printed insoles built to digital gait data
    • Bunion (hallux valgus) offloading and flat foot support
    • Overuse knee, hip, and shin splint biomechanics

    • Clear clinical milestones and gait retraining
