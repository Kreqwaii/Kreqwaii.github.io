Diagnostic Engineering

Why 3D CBCT & Digital Wax-Up Preview Come First

Complex rehabilitative and implant treatment succeeds in the planning room before any tooth preparation or placement begins.

STAGE 01 · VOLUMETRIC SCAN

3D CBCT Bone & Anatomy Mapping

Three-dimensional imaging reveals bone thickness, sinus proximity, and root health across every millimeter, guiding whether implants can be placed directly or benefit from staged grafting.

STAGE 02 · DIGITAL WAX-UP

Trial Smile & Occlusal Architecture

Optical scans merge with facial measurements to sculpt your prospective tooth forms digitally. A physical mock-up lets you preview comfort, speech, and aesthetics in advance.

STAGE 03 · PROVISIONAL PHASE

Verified Function Prior to Final Ceramics

During multi-unit rehabilitation or implant healing, custom provisionals allow real-world testing of your bite before master lab ceramics are milled and bonded.
