Clinical Capabilities

Veterinary services at River City Veterinary Hospital

Every appointment starts with a calm, unhurried physical assessment and clear communication about recommended next steps.

Pet Wellness Exams & Vaccinations

Comprehensive annual and semi-annual wellness exams for puppies, kittens, adult companions, and senior pets. Includes individualized core and lifestyle vaccinations, heartworm and tick-borne screening, nutrition counseling, and microchipping.

Nose-to-Tail Exam Tailored Vaccines Senior Screening
Typical visit: 30 – 45 min

Veterinary Surgery & Dental Care

Ultrasonic dental scaling, subgingival cleaning, polishing, and dental radiographs alongside soft-tissue surgical procedures. Each anesthetic patient receives pre-op bloodwork, IV fluid support, active warming, and dedicated vitals monitoring.

Ultrasonic Dental Oral X-Rays Soft-Tissue Surgery
Drop-off care & written surgical quote

Diagnostic Imaging & Lab Testing

Rapid in-house blood chemistry, complete blood count (CBC), urinalysis, cytology, digital radiography, and focused abdominal ultrasound to identify underlying conditions quickly without unnecessary delays.

Digital X-Rays In-House Lab Focused Ultrasound
Same-visit lab turnaround

Urgent Pet Care

Daytime urgent evaluations for sudden illness, gastrointestinal distress, minor wounds, ear infections, allergic reactions, or acute lameness. Call ahead so our triage team can prepare for your pet upon arrival.

Same-Day Triage Pain & Fluid Relief Point-of-Care Labs
Priority daytime appointments

    What to Bring

    Bring prior immunization records, current prescription labels or supplements, and a fresh fecal sample if your pet is due for annual parasite screening. Please use a secure carrier for cats and a short non-retractable leash for dogs.

    After examining your pet, your veterinarian reviews findings and provides an itemized estimate before drawing blood, taking radiographs, or beginning dental or surgical procedures.
