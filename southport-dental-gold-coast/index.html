Core Clinical Care

Five Dedicated Services Under One Roof

Calm, unhurried Gold Coast private care tailored for working professionals, families, and patients seeking restorative precision or better sleep.

Digital CAD/CAM Suite

Same Day In-House Crown (CEREC)

Skip the traditional two-week laboratory wait and delicate plastic temporary crowns. Using digital intraoral optical scanning and our in-house ceramic milling unit, we craft, glaze, and permanently bond custom tooth-coloured crowns in a single appointment.

1 Visit · ~90 mins · $1,850 AUD guide
Permanent Tooth Replacement

Dental Implants

Restore natural biting strength and balanced facial support following tooth loss. From single-tooth implant crowns to multi-unit restorative planning, every phase is digitally mapped for bone integration and natural gum-line aesthetics.

Staged Care · $4,650 AUD complete guide

Zoom Teeth Whitening

In-chair Zoom whitening safely lifts deep coffee, wine, and age-related staining in around 75 minutes, with desensitising mineral care and optional custom home trays.
