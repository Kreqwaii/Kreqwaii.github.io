Project Specification & Estimating Questions

How do Wasatch Front snow loads affect roof system selection and pricing? +
Valley floor cities such as Murray and Salt Lake City typically design around 30–40 psf ground snow loads, while East Bench and Canyon communities escalate to 50–100+ psf. Higher snow zones require denser fastener patterns, continuous double-course self-adhered ice-and-water membrane at eaves and valleys, thicker structural polyiso cover boards on flat roofs, and clamped snow-retention bars on standing seam metal.
When should a Salt Lake commercial building choose Single-Ply TPO vs. Modified Bitumen or BUR? +
Single-ply 60-mil or 80-mil TPO excels on wide-span warehouses and retail buildings prioritizing solar reflectivity, rapid heat-welded seams, and clean tapered insulation drainage. Multi-ply Modified Bitumen or Built-Up Roofing (BUR) is frequently specified where heavy rooftop HVAC servicing, frequent contractor foot traffic, or puncture redundancy is critical.
Does D-7 Roofing handle both commercial industrial facilities and residential homes out of Murray? +
Yes. Our Salt Lake City regional office at 4222 South 500 West in Murray operates specialized steep-slope residential crews (architectural shingles, concrete/clay tile, standing seam metal) alongside commercial low-slope crews (TPO, EPDM, modified bitumen, and built-up systems).
