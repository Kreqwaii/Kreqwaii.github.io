Clinical Disciplines

Four specialist pillars under one Barnes roof

Our clinicians collaborate across disciplines so you are matched with the right assessment and treatment pathway from your first visit.

MSK & Sports Physiotherapy

Biomechanical assessment, tendon loading programmes, and post-operative rehabilitation for Richmond Park runners, rowers, and active families.

  • ACL & knee rehab
  • Achilles & plantar pain
  • Shoulder impingement
  • Post-surgical recovery
Initial 45 min assessment £95

Osteopathy & Cranial Osteopathy

Whole-body structural osteopathy for acute back lock, neck tension, and postural strain, alongside gentle cranial osteopathy for infants and adults.

  • Lower back & sciatica
  • Cervical & jaw tension
  • Cranial osteopathy
  • Joint articulation
Initial 45 min assessment £95

Acupuncture & Manual Therapy

Western medical acupuncture and dry needling integrated into physiotherapy or osteopathic sessions to settle myofascial trigger points and neural sensitivity.
