On-Site Standard

What happens during your free Manassas roof inspection.

Clear photographic evidence, honest repair-versus-replacement advice, and zero high-pressure sales tactics.

STEP 01

Perimeter & Slope Survey

We inspect drip edges, gutter pitch, fascia soundness, shingle granule retention, or commercial TPO seam welds with high-resolution documentation.

STEP 02

Attic & Decking Check

Inside the attic, we check sheathing moisture stains, nail penetrations, soffit intake clearance, and bathroom exhaust venting routes.

STEP 03

Written Line-Item Scope

You receive an itemized specification covering underlayment weight, flashing gauge, ventilation math, and transparent USD pricing.

STEP 04

Protected Build & Cleanup

Landscaping tarps, continuous debris containment, rolling magnetic nail sweeps, and final walkthrough sign-off with our project lead.
