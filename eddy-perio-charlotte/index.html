    3D Guided Surgery

    Dental Implants

    Biocompatible titanium implant placement planned with CBCT 3D bone volume imaging, socket preservation or ridge augmentation where indicated, and seamless coordination with your restorative dentist for the final custom crown.

    Healing: 12 - 20 Weeks
    Tissue & Bone Health

    Periodontal Treatment

    Targeted nonsurgical scaling and root planing, pocket depth reduction surgery, guided bone regeneration, and soft tissue grafting designed to arrest periodontal progression and stabilize natural dentition.

    Evaluation: 4 - 12 Weeks
    Minimally Invasive

    Laser Periodontal Therapy

    Selective laser wavelength energy targets pathogenic bacteria and inflamed pocket epithelium while preserving healthy gum architecture, promoting gentle suture-free clot stabilization and comfortable post-visit recovery.

    Recovery: 3 - 14 Days
    Restorative & Esthetic

    Crown Lengthening

    Precise recontouring of gingival tissue and supporting crestal bone to expose sound tooth structure for durable dental crowns or harmonize an asymmetric gum line prior to cosmetic dentistry.

    Maturation: 6 - 12 Weeks
