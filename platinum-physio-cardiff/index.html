    Meet Your Lead Clinician

    P. Leighton Jones

    MManipTher · BSc (Hons) · MCSP · HCPC Registered
    Founder · Est. 2005

    Born and raised in Carmarthen and trained in Cardiff, Leighton qualified from the University of Wales, College of Medicine in 1997. In 2002 he completed the internationally recognised Master of Manipulative Therapy degree at Curtin University in Perth, Western Australia, specialising in complex and persistent spinal conditions, occupational posture, and elite sports rehabilitation.

    • Peer-Reviewed Shoulder Research: Presented at the World Physiotherapy Congress 2003 (Barcelona) and published in Manual Therapy 10 (2005) 191-197: “Reliability of palpation of humeral head position in asymptomatic shoulders”.
    • International & Professional Sport: Clinical experience with the Great Britain Bobsleigh Team, Welsh Ladies Hockey Team, Celtic Warriors regional rugby, and Great Britain Ultimate Frisbee.
    • Complex & Second-Opinion Cases: Many patients arrive after unsuccessful treatment elsewhere; extended 45-minute appointments allow thorough biomechanical diagnosis.
