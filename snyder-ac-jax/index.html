Northeast Florida Sizing & Multi-Trade Engineering Notes

Climate Zone 2A

Latent Humidity & Equipment Staging

Jacksonville summers pair high afternoon temperatures with coastal dew points. Oversized single-stage AC equipment reaches thermostat setpoint quickly without running long enough to pull moisture out of indoor air. Variable-speed or dedicated whole-home dehumidification helps hold indoor relative humidity between 45% and 50%.

Airflow & Ducts

Attic Static Pressure & Sealing

Unsealed trunk transitions, crushed flex runs, and undersized return plenums force blower motors to work against excessive static pressure while drawing humid attic air into conditioned rooms. Measuring total external static pressure alongside cooling load avoids premature compressor wear.

HVAC, Plumbing & Electrical

Coordinated Home Infrastructure

Condensate drainage, main drain clearing, water heater expansion control, and HVAC electrical disconnects intersect across every mechanical room. Coordinating AC Installation & Repair, Mini-Split & Ductwork Service, Plumbing & Drain Clearing, and Indoor Air Quality & Dehumidifiers reduces repeat dispatch trips.
