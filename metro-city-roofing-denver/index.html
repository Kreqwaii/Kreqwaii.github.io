How Our Denver Inspection & Replacement Process Works

A disciplined three-step workflow designed to protect your home, verify storm damage accurately, and eliminate guesswork.

STEP 01 · FORENSIC AUDIT

18-Point Hail & Soft-Metal Inspection

We inspect 10x10 test squares on every directional slope, check gutters, vents, and flashing for spatter and dents, and deliver a clear photo-documented assessment.

STEP 02 · SCOPE & PERMITTING

Code-Verified Scope & Material Selection

We prepare a line-item replacement specification covering ice-and-water shield, drip edge, ventilation, and Class 4 UL-2218 impact certificates for your carrier.

STEP 03 · PRECISION INSTALL

One-Day Tear-Off & Final Walkthrough

Dedicated Front Range crews complete tear-off, decking inspection, installation, magnetic nail sweep, and final municipal inspection sign-off.
