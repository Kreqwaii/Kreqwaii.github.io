Your First Visit & Answers

What to Expect at Orleans Physiotherapy

Wear comfortable clothing such as shorts or a tank top so your therapist can examine the area easily (changing facilities are available on site), and bring any imaging reports or insurance details.

STEP 01

Intake & History

Arrive a few minutes early for your intake form, then review symptom history, lifestyle, past injuries, and goals one-on-one.

STEP 02

Physical Assessment

Your therapist evaluates posture, joint range of motion, muscle strength, balance, and functional movement to identify the root cause.

STEP 03

Initial Treatment

In most cases, your first visit includes hands-on manual therapy, cold laser, or acupuncture right away in your private room.

STEP 04

Home Plan & Follow-Ups

Leave with clear exercises and schedule 50 to 60 minute follow-ups around early mornings, evenings, or Saturday hours.
