    When should I upgrade from a Regular boiler to a Combi? +

    For 1–2 bathroom flats, terraces or semis, converting to a Combi frees airing-cupboard and loft tank space while supplying mains-pressure hot water. Homes with 3+ bathrooms running simultaneously often perform better with a System boiler and cylinder.

    What is included in an annual boiler service? +

    We inspect flue integrity, test combustion ratios with a calibrated analyser, verify gas burner pressure, clean condensate traps and magnetic filters, check expansion vessel charge and confirm all safety devices operate correctly.

    Do I need a powerflush with a new boiler in Norwich? +

    Older radiator circuits in Norfolk commonly build up black magnetite sludge and limescale. Flushing the system and fitting a magnetic filter protects your new boiler heat exchanger and keeps manufacturer warranties valid.
