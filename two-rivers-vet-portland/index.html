Core Veterinary Services

Thoughtful medicine across every stage of life.

From first puppy and kitten visits to senior dental health and integrative mobility support, every treatment plan at Two Rivers Veterinary Clinic prioritizes gentle handling and clear pricing.

Wellness Care

Comprehensive nose-to-tail exams, individualized vaccine timing, year-round parasite protection, and early organ screening.

  • Puppy, kitten, adult & senior exams
  • PNW Leptospirosis & vector coverage
  • Annual & senior preventive blood panels

Routine Surgery

Safe spay, neuter, and soft-tissue procedures supported by pre-anesthetic lab work, IV fluids, and multi-modal pain relief.

  • Individualized anesthesia monitoring
  • Heated recovery & pain control
  • Spay, neuter & dermal mass excision

Dental Care

Ultrasonic scaling, subgingival cleaning, full-mouth digital dental X-rays, and gentle oral surgery when painful teeth are found.

  • Full-mouth digital dental radiography
  • Periodontal charting & root evaluation
  • Regional nerve blocks for comfort

Alternative Therapies

Integrative care blending veterinary acupuncture, Class IV laser therapy, and targeted joint nutrition for smoother movement.

  • Fine-needle veterinary acupuncture
  • Class IV laser photobiomodulation
  • Arthritis & post-surgical rehabilitation
