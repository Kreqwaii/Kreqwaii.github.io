Field Methodology

How Every Service Call Is Engineered

No guesswork parts swapping. Every repair, audit, and system replacement follows a documented measurement workflow.

STEP 01

Static Pressure & Load Check

Measure supply/return static pressure and temperature split across the coil or heat exchanger to isolate airflow bottlenecks.

STEP 02

Combustion & Refrigerant Telemetry

Log superheat, subcooling, amp draw, or gas manifold pressure and flue CO before recommending component action.

STEP 03

Upfront Scope & SEER ROI

Review exact repair pricing versus multi-year energy payback on high-efficiency upgrades with clear written options.

STEP 04

Commissioning Sign-Off

Verify target delta-T, balanced register airflow, and thermostat staging under real load prior to technician departure.
