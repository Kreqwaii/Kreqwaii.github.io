Meet Dr. Sarah Kong & Philosophy

Listening closely before recommending conservative dental care.

Dallas Dental Wellness treats the mouth as the gateway to whole-body health, pairing unhurried consultation time with evidence-based biomimetic dentistry.

SK

Dr. Sarah Kong, DDS

Founder · Baylor College of Dentistry Clinical Instructor

Born in Dallas and raised in Park Cities, Dr. Kong trained at Baylor College of Dentistry and spent more than a decade in private practice in the Dallas Arts District alongside leading specialists before opening Dallas Dental Wellness in Preston Center.

An artist at heart with a discerning eye for proportion, she teaches future dentists at her alma mater, publishes clinical research with colleagues, and devotes unhurried time so every patient feels heard without judgement.

  • Faculty Mentor & Clinical Instructor, Baylor College of Dentistry (Dallas, TX)
  • Published dental journal author across conservative & esthetic techniques
  • Hundreds of continuing education hours in Cosmetics, Implants, Orthodontics & Sedation
  • Active committee leadership across local, state, and national dental boards
