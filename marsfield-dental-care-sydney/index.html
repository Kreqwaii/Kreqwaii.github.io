Australian Private Extras Guide

How HICAPS & Annual Limits Work at Reception

Clear financial guidance helps you plan restorative and cosmetic work without surprises on treatment day.

01 / ON-THE-SPOT CLAIMING

Instant HICAPS Terminal Processing

Bring your physical or digital health fund membership card to 52 Waring Street. Our reception team claims your benefit electronically so you settle only the out-of-pocket gap.

02 / PRE-TREATMENT CHECKS

Written ADA Itemised Quotes

Before commencing Major Dental care such as Dental Implants or Crowns & Bridges, we provide exact ADA item numbers so you can confirm your exact dollar rebate with your fund.

03 / LIMIT OPTIMISATION

Staged Care Across Reset Dates

Multi-month procedures such as Dental Implants naturally split into surgical and crown stages, allowing eligible patients to align billing across calendar or financial year limit resets.
