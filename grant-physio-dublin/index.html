Principal Physiotherapist & Olympic Heritage

Dr. Marie-Elaine Grant

A graduate of University College Dublin with over three decades in clinical practice, Dr. Marie-Elaine Grant combines international sports medicine leadership with attentive, unhurried one-to-one physiotherapy in Blackrock.

While widely recognised for leading physiotherapy services for Ireland's Olympic athletes, her clinical philosophy serves patients across every stage of life: from office workers recovering from persistent low back pain and older adults rebuilding strength after hip or knee replacement, to competitive athletes aiming for measurable return to sport.

1990 to 2010 Lead Physiotherapist, Team Ireland: Directed physiotherapy care across five Summer and two Winter Olympic Games.
2010 to Present IOC Medical & Science Commission: Appointed to plan, deliver, and evaluate Olympic medical and physiotherapy services worldwide.
UCD D.Sc. (Hon.) Honorary Doctorate & Research: Awarded by University College Dublin alongside peer-reviewed clinical publications and postgraduate mentoring.
2024 RCSI Award FFSEM (Hon.): Honorary Fellowship from the Faculty of Sports and Exercise Medicine at RCSI following the Paris 2024 Olympic Games.
Evidence-Based Clinical Services

Six specialised pillars of recovery, movement, and measurable strength

Every treatment plan is delivered one-to-one by experienced Chartered Physiotherapists working closely with orthopaedic surgeons and medical colleagues across Dublin.

Manual Therapy

Soft tissue treatment and specialised joint and spinal mobilisation techniques following injury or surgery to reduce pain, release restriction, and restore pain-free range of motion.

Joint & Soft Tissue

Post-Op & Orthopaedic Rehab

Structured rehabilitation after hip and knee replacement surgery, ACL reconstruction, and orthopaedic procedures, guiding safe progression from early recovery to full daily function.

Hip, Knee & ACL

Sports Injury Management

Biomechanical and mechanical screening for recreational and elite athletes to resolve acute sprains, tendon overload, movement dysfunction, and training-load errors.

Recreational to Elite

Back Pain & Core Stability

Individualised one-to-one spinal rehabilitation for acute, sub-acute, and persistent back pain, uniting spinal stabilisation, core strength, posture correction, and movement retraining.

Spinal Health

Isokinetic Evaluation (Biodex)

Objective Biodex muscle strength, endurance, and limb-symmetry testing alongside grip strength and functional metrics to guide safe loading and return-to-sport readiness.

Measurable Outcomes

Persistent Pain & Modalities

Comprehensive care for chronic musculoskeletal pain, balance and proprioceptive re-education, postural assessment, electrotherapy modalities, and clinical taping techniques.

Long-Term Function
