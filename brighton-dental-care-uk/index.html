Clinical Disciplines

Four focused pillars of private dental care.

Every clinical pathway at 69-75 Dyke Rd begins with minimally invasive preservation, digital diagnostics, and clear written fee schedules in GBP before treatment commences.

01 · GENERAL & PREVENTIVE

General & Preventive Dentistry

Comprehensive oral health reviews, soft-tissue screening, low-dose digital radiographs, and proactive caries prevention tailored to adult and family patients.

  • 45-minute New Patient Examination
  • Digital occlusal and periodontal charting
  • Written preventive recall schedule
New Patient Exam from £85
02 · COSMETIC

Cosmetic Dentistry

Enamel-conscious aesthetic improvements designed around natural tooth anatomy, measured shade progression, and conservative composite bonding.

  • Clinician-monitored take-home whitening
  • Multi-shade anterior composite contouring
  • Pre-cosmetic gum health stabilisation
Whitening Programme £380
03 · RESTORATIVE

Restorative Treatments

Structural rehabilitation using tooth-coloured biomimetic composites, laboratory-crafted porcelain crowns, and staged occlusal restoration plans.

  • Bonded white composite fillings
  • Full-ceramic & zirconia crowns
  • Structured Restorative Care Plans
Fillings £175 · Crowns £790
04 · HYGIENE & ORAL HEALTH

Hygiene & Oral Health

Warm-water Guided Biofilm Therapy and Airflow polishing to remove extrinsic staining and subgingival plaque gently, protecting natural teeth and restorations.

  • Gentle Airflow warm-water stain removal
  • Periodontal pocket maintenance
  • Bespoke interdental care guidance
Hygiene Airflow £95
