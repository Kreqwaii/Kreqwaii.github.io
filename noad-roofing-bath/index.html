BATH HERITAGE & LISTED BUILDINGS

            
Listed Building Consent & Conservation Craftsmanship

            

              Bath contains one of the highest densities of Grade I, Grade II* and Grade II listed roofs in Britain. Replacing natural slate or clay tiles with incompatible modern alternatives or altering dormers and chimneys normally requires formal Listed Building Consent through Bath & North East Somerset Council.
            

            

              Noad Roofing supports homeowners and managing agents with like-for-like material matching, salvaged Bath slate and clay tile sourcing, LSTA sand-cast or milled leadwork, and drone photographic evidence prior to specification.
            


            

              

                
Like-for-Like Slate & Clay

                
Blending reclaimed Bath tiles and natural slate seamlessly into existing heritage slopes.

              

              

                
Drone & Internal Loft Surveys

                
Aerial photographic assessment paired with internal batten, felt and timber inspection.
