Preparing for Your Triage Appointment

  • Note which tooth or quadrant is affected, how long discomfort has lasted, and whether hot, cold, or biting triggers pain.
  • Bring a current list of regular medications and any known allergies to your assessment at Guildford Dental Clinic.
  • If a crown, bridge, or veneer has detached, store it safely in a rigid clean container and bring it along without using household glues.
  • Written private treatment options and transparent GBP fees are always agreed with you before definitive work begins.
