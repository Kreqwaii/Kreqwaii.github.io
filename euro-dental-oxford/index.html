NHS Free Treatment Exemptions

Free NHS dental care applies if you are aged under 18, under 19 in full-time education, pregnant or had a baby within the past 12 months (with valid MatEx, MATB1, or birth certificate), or hold qualifying exemptions including Income Support, Universal Credit criteria, Pension Credit guarantee credit, or a valid HC2 certificate.
