Payment & Fee Transparency

Three clear ways to structure your care.

Compare PPO insurance benefit estimates, direct self-pay savings, and 12-month installment budgeting side by side before scheduling.

PPO Insurance

Our office verifies preventive, diagnostic, and major restorative PPO coverage prior to treatment. Preventive exams and emergency diagnostics frequently carry minimal out-of-pocket cost, while elective cosmetic veneers and whitening are typically self-funded.

Self-Pay Courtesy

Patients without PPO coverage or choosing elective cosmetic care receive a 10% direct-pay courtesy reduction when settling standard treatment fees at the time of service.

12-Month Financing

Break larger restorative, implant, or veneer treatment plans into 12 equal monthly USD installments so needed dental care remains predictable alongside household budgeting.
