Post-Visit Enamel Protection

48-Hour White-Diet Aftercare Timeline

Enamel remains slightly dehydrated and receptive to pigments for 48 hours after cold-LED whitening. This timeline automatically adapts when you select Mineral Serum or Teeth Gems above.

Hours 0 – 2

Pure Water Rehydration

Drink only room-temperature still water while enamel pores begin to close and salivary minerals re-equilibrate.

Standard protocol: Avoid hot coffee, ice water, and coloured mouthwash.
Hours 2 – 24

Strict White Shirt Rule

Consume only light-coloured foods and dairy. If a food or drink would stain a crisp white linen shirt, postpone it past hour 48.

Studio check: Add the Mineral Serum Kit above for evening tubule sealing.
Hours 24 – 48

Guided Reintroduction

Reintroduce mild warm foods, use a straw for light iced tea or coffee, and rinse thoroughly with plain water immediately after meals.

Gentle care: Use a soft-bristle brush and non-charcoal toothpaste.
Day 3 Onward

Long-Term Shade Lock

Enamel cuticle resilience returns to baseline. Maintain peak brightness by rinsing after red wine or booking a 40-min Refresh every 6–9 months.

Maintenance ready: Touch-Up Pen optional for heavy espresso routines.

Client Questions

Whitening & Gem Studio FAQ

Whitening gels lift organic stains strictly within natural tooth enamel. Porcelain crowns, veneers, and composite fillings stay at their existing fabricated colour. During your Cosmetic Smile Consultation, we check visible front restorations so you know what shade range blends evenly.
We coat exposed gumlines with a protective resin seal and customize gel formulation by reactivity. Selecting Mild or High Sensitivity Guard adds potassium nitrate buffering, gentle cycling intervals, and Nano-Hydroxyapatite mineral sealing to reduce transient cold zingers.
Zero drilling and zero injections are used. Each crystal or 18K solid gold charm is bonded onto clean enamel using dental-grade light-cured composite resin, similar to orthodontic bracket placement. Gems typically last 6 to 18 months and can be safely polished off by a dental professional anytime.
Whitening first ensures the enamel underneath your crystal or 18K gold charm matches your newly brightened smile. Booking both in one visit at our Calgary studio also qualifies for the automatic $20 CAD Same-Day Whitening + Gem Bundle Credit.
