Dental Esthetique Clinical Scope

Four Core Services in Subiaco

Every service connects directly with the gap-free and rebate estimator above so patients can compare preventative upkeep alongside cosmetic and restorative options.

General Dentistry

Preventative check-ups, ultrasonic scale and clean, diagnostic digital X-rays, gum health monitoring, and tooth-coloured restorative care.

  • Comprehensive new-patient assessments
  • Six-monthly scale, polish & fluoride
  • Preventative focus for lifelong enamel
$0 Gap Eligible*

Teeth Whitening

Dentist-supervised whitening tailored for minimal sensitivity, offering custom fitted take-home trays or single-visit clinical brightening.

  • Shade assessment & enamel suitability check
  • In-chair clinical LED brightening sessions
  • Custom fitted trays for gradual brightness
From $390 AUD

Dental Implants

Fixed single-tooth and multi-unit tooth replacement designed to restore natural biting strength and balanced gingival aesthetics.

  • 3D volumetric CBCT bone assessment
  • Staged titanium fixture & healing phase
  • Custom shaded porcelain crown finish
From $4,850 AUD

Invisalign & Orthodontics

Discreet removable clear aligners planned using digital 3D intraoral scanning to guide tooth movement comfortably without brackets.

  • Digital 3D arch scan & movement preview
  • Express, Moderate & Comprehensive plans
  • Post-treatment retention planning
From $4,400 AUD
