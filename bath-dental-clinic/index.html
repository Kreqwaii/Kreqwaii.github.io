Clinical Disciplines

Specialist and general care under one roof

Patients may attend directly for general care, cosmetic alignment, and hygiene, or be referred by their dentist or medical practitioner for complex restorative, endodontic, and implant rehabilitation.

Straumann Dental Implants

Single-tooth replacement, implant overdentures, soft tissue grafting, guided bone regeneration, and sinus lift augmentation planned with 3D CBCT diagnostics.

From £130.00

Cosmetic & Restorative Dentistry

Composite bonding, ceramic veneers (£1,100 per unit), bespoke crowns, adhesive bridges, teeth whitening, and full-mouth occlusal rehabilitation.

POA Assessment

ClearCorrect Aligners

Discreet transparent aligner therapy designed for gradual tooth movement without fixed metal brackets, removable for meals and daily oral hygiene.

From £750.00

Specialist Endodontics

Dedicated root canal treatment, complex retreatments, management of cracked teeth, and internal bleaching led by Rebecca Kirke MSc Endo (KCL).

From £185.00

Paediatric Dentistry & Sedation

Consultant-led care for anxious or young patients under Miss Sarah Tukmachi MPaedDent RCS, with inhalational and intravenous conscious sedation options.

From £85.00

Preventive Hygiene & TMD

Direct Access Hygiene with Katy Colbourne, periodontal disease stabilisation, Michigan and Tanner occlusal splints (£975), and chronic orofacial pain management.
