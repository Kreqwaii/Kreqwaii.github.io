    Clinical Scope

    General, cosmetic and restorative care under one roof

    Every consultation is led by a principal practitioner, combining preventative diagnostics, aesthetic craftsmanship, and conservative restoration without rushed appointments.

    Preventative Check-ups & Screening

    Comprehensive dental examinations including oral cancer screening, instant digital panoramic (OPG), bitewing, and periapical X-rays, plus fissure sealants for younger teeth.

    • Standard Exam: €40 · PRSI Exam: €0
    • Scale & Polish: €60 · PRSI: €15
    • Child Exam (under 12): €40

    Tooth-Coloured Fillings & Endodontics

    Replace ageing silver amalgam fillings with natural white composite or reinforced glass ionomer restorations, alongside careful root canal therapy for incisors, premolars, and molars.

    • Composite Fillings: €100 to €150
    • Root Canal: €450 to €600 (Med 2 eligible)
    • Periodontal deep scaling: €120 per session

    Extractions & Urgent Repairs

    Prompt assessment for sudden toothache, fractured incisors, or lost fillings, as well as routine, surgical, and wisdom tooth extractions under gentle local anaesthesia.

    • Minimum emergency / dressing fee: €50
    • Normal Extraction: €100 · Surgical: €160
    • Wisdom Tooth Extraction: €160 to €250

    Composite Bonding in One Visit

    Our least invasive cosmetic technique. Shade-matched resin is bonded to cleaned enamel, cured with UV light, and sculpted in a single appointment without injections or drilling away healthy tooth structure.

    • €250 per tooth for full composite veneer
    • Closes gaps, repairs chipped edges, and brightens tooth shade
    • Pain-free procedure suitable across all adult age groups

    3-Step Smile Makeover & Whitening

    Our controlled teeth whitening system achieves shades up to 4 times lighter, removing tea, coffee, and lifestyle staining before or alongside subtle cosmetic tooth and gum contouring.

    • Complete Upper & Lower Whitening: €375
    • Step 1: Assessment & budget planning
    • Step 2: Combined whitening, bonding, contouring or ceramic plan
    • Step 3: Sequenced treatment visits with clear cost breakdown

    Porcelain & Emax Veneers

    Ultra-thin porcelain shells crafted by specialist ceramists. Following a diagnostic wax-up and temporary trial smile, teeth typically need only 0.5 to 0.75mm enamel preparation under local anaesthetic.

    • Porcelain Veneers: €800 (€650 - €800 case guide)
    • Natural translucency with no dark gum-line margin
    • 20% Revenue Med 2 tax relief available

    Ceramic & Zirconia Crowns & Bridges

    Full-coverage Emax and Porcelain-fused-to-Zirconia crowns rebuild heavily filled, fractured, or worn teeth, while bespoke ceramic bridges close single or multi-tooth gaps cleanly.

    • Crowns: €800 · Inlay / Onlay: €550
    • Titanium / Ceramic post & core: €150
    • 20% Med 2 relief on crowns and bridges

    Dental Implants (Free Consultation)

    Biocompatible titanium implants integrate with the jawbone over approximately 12 weeks before attaching an abutment and lifelike crown to replace missing teeth permanently.

    • Systems used: Nobel Biocare, Southern Implants, Thommen Implants
    • Free clinical implant consultation (POA)
    • 20% Revenue Med 2 tax relief eligible

    Custom Athletic & Teen Mouthguards

    Custom-fitted from your personal dental impressions for optimal breathing, speech, and impact protection. Available in club colours with a complimentary 90-day clinical check-up included.

    • Sports Mouthguard: €100 (Free 90-day check-up)
    • Hard / Soft Night Guard Splints: €175 to €250
    • Emergency tip: Hold knocked-out teeth by the crown only, store in milk or saline, and attend immediately

    Same-Day Clear Essix Retainers & Dentures

    Protect orthodontic alignment immediately when an aligner or retainer cracks or goes missing, or restore full function with precision acrylic and cobalt-chrome dentures.

    • Same-day Clear Essix Retainers: €100 per arch
    • Acrylic Dentures: €400 to €600
    • Cobalt Chrome Dentures: €1,200
