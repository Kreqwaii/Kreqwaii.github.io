Clinical Services

General & Preventive Dentistry

Routine exams, gentle cleanings, and conservative restorations to protect natural enamel.

  • Comprehensive oral exams and low-dose digital X-rays
  • Adult hygiene cleanings and periodontal maintenance
  • Tooth-colored composite fillings matched to natural shade
Typical fee: $145 – $360

Cosmetic Dentistry & Teeth Whitening

Measured shade brightening and conservative porcelain aesthetics balanced with facial proportions.

  • In-office desensitizing LED teeth whitening
  • Custom-fitted porcelain veneers and bonding
  • Digital shade mapping for predictable symmetry
Typical fee: $490 – $1,650

Dental Implants & Crowns

Durable structural tooth replacement and full-coverage ceramics engineered for daily comfort.

  • Single-tooth titanium implants and custom abutments
  • All-ceramic and zirconia dental crowns
  • Bite calibration for natural chewing stability
Typical fee: $1,380 – $3,850

Emergency Dental Care

Prompt Minneapolis triage for acute pain, broken teeth, lost crowns, and localized swelling.

  • Focused emergency diagnostic exam and X-ray
  • Immediate pain stabilization and temporary repairs
  • Targeted root canal therapy when nerve care is needed
Typical fee: $160 – $1,420
