Nervous Patient Care

Designed for patients who have avoided the dentist for years.

A significant proportion of our Bristol patients come to Queen Square specifically for our calm environment and medically supervised sedation protocols.

01 / CONSULTATION FIRST

No-rush first conversation

Your first visit can take place without any clinical instruments if preferred. We discuss your concerns, review digital scans, and agree on a pace that feels manageable.

02 / IV SEDATION

Deep relaxation, awake and safe

Administered carefully via a small cannula in the hand or arm, intravenous sedation induces a calm, drowsy state with little memory of the procedure itself.

03 / TRANSPARENT PRICING

Written GBP fee plans

Every patient receives a clear, itemised treatment plan and 0% APR finance schedule before committing to any appointments.
