    GEELONG FAMILY GUIDANCE

    Making the most of 6-month recalls, CDBS, and health fund limits.

    Why 6-Month Family Recalls Work

    Six-monthly checkups and cleans catch early enamel demineralisation and tartar build-up before small spots turn into toothache or larger restorations. Grouping adult and child appointments into a single family block helps everyone keep the same rhythm.

    Medicare CDBS for Eligible Kids (0–17)

    The Child Dental Benefits Schedule provides eligible Australian children aged 0–17 with benefits across two consecutive calendar years for basic dental services including checkups, cleans, fissure sealants, and fillings. Custom sports mouthguards are not covered by CDBS.

    Private Health Fund Extras Reset Timing

    Many Australian health funds reset annual extras limits on 1 January or 1 July and offer gap-free or high-rebate preventive checkups every 6 months. Scheduling checkups across both halves of your benefit year prevents unused preventive entitlements from lapsing.
