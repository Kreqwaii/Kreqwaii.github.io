Colorado Law & Claim Transparency

Important Deductible Rule for Colorado Roof Claims

Under Colorado law, it is illegal for any roofing contractor to waive, rebate, or absorb all or part of your property insurance deductible. Contractors promising a free roof without collecting your policy deductible expose homeowners to insurance fraud.

Apex Roofing USA does not guarantee claim approval or determine policy coverage. We provide factual storm documentation, transparent scope guidance, and collect deductibles strictly as required by your insurance agreement.

Colorado Roofing & Claim Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear factual answers about Colorado hail inspections, deductible rules, TPO vs EPDM, and GAF System Plus warranties.

Does hail always cause visible roof leaks right away? +
No. Hail frequently bruises shingle fiberglass mats, dislodges protective ceramic granules, and dents gutters or soft metals months before water reaches interior drywall. A slope-by-slope inspection documents impact early while storm claim windows remain open.
Should I get a roof inspection before filing an insurance claim? +
Yes. A $0 inspection with CompanyCam GPS photos and a HailTrace weather history check tells you whether visible storm damage justifies filing a claim or whether minor maintenance or no claim makes better financial sense.
What is the difference between TPO and EPDM for commercial roofs in Denver? +
Both are single-ply low-slope membranes. TPO is a reflective light-colored membrane with heat-welded seams that helps lower summer cooling demand under intense Denver sunlight. EPDM is a durable black rubber membrane known for long-term cold-weather flexibility and straightforward patching.
Does Colorado have a statewide roofing contractor license? +
No. Colorado does not license roofers at the state level; contractor licensing and building permits are managed by each city or county. Apex pulls municipal permits in Denver, Englewood, Aurora, Lakewood, and surrounding Front Range jurisdictions and closes every job with a city final inspection.
What warranties come with a new Apex Roofing installation? +
New roof installations include a 5-year Apex Roofing labor warranty alongside manufacturer coverage. As a GAF Certified™ contractor (GAF ID 1005038), Apex is authorized to offer the enhanced GAF System Plus Limited Warranty on qualifying complete GAF roof systems.
