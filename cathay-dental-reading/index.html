    Orthodontic Clarity

    How Smilelign Clear Braces progress

    A predictable four-stage clinical pathway from initial digital assessment at Cathay House through to long-term retention.

    01

    Clinical & Digital Scan

    We examine tooth position, gum health, and bite balance, capturing precise digital records without traditional impression trays.

    02

    3D Treatment Preview

    Review your projected tooth movements stage by stage and confirm exact timescales and finance terms before aligners are crafted.

    03

    Sequential Clear Aligners

    Wear removable crystal-clear trays for 20–22 hours daily, stepping into the next aligner roughly every two weeks with 6-weekly clinic reviews.

    04

    Retention & Finishing

    Once alignment is complete, retainers preserve your result, with optional whitening or edge composite refinement if desired.
