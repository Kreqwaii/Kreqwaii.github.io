IAOMT SMART Protocol Safety Barriers

Mercury vaporizes rapidly when disturbed by standard dental burs. Our SMART protocol eliminates unnecessary exposure for both patient and clinical team during amalgam replacement.

  • Non-latex nitrile rubber dam isolation combined with oral aerosol vacuuming directly at the tooth
  • External nasal oxygen supply and charcoal/chlorella rinse protocol before and after sectioning
  • Sectioning in large chunks under copious chilled water with high-capacity IQAir mercury filtration
