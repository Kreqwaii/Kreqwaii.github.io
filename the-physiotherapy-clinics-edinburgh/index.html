Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation

Comprehensive orthopaedic and spinal assessment targeting joint stiffness, nerve irritation, and progressive functional restoration.

  • Lower back, sciatica, cervical neck, and thoracic pain
  • Post-operative hip, knee, and shoulder joint protocols
  • Targeted exercise prescription with measurable milestones

45–60 min

Sports Injuries

Load-based rehabilitation for runners, club athletes, and active Edinburgh residents recovering from ligament, tendon, or muscle injury.

  • Achilles, patellar, and gluteal tendinopathy loading
  • ACL, ankle sprain, and hamstring return-to-sport staging
  • Objective strength symmetry and agility clearance checks
Initial £80 · Follow-up £65
45–60 min

Vestibular Rehabilitation

Specialist inner-ear and balance rehabilitation designed to resolve vertigo, visual-motion dizziness, and unsteady walking patterns.

  • Diagnostic BPPV canalith repositioning manoeuvres
  • Vestibulo-ocular reflex (VOR) gaze stabilisation training
  • Graded habituation for post-concussion and balance deficits
Initial £95 · Follow-up £72
45–60 min

Massage Therapy

Clinical soft-tissue release and targeted sports massage supporting tissue mobility, training recovery, and muscular tension relief.

  • Deep myofascial release and trigger-point decompression
  • Pre-event priming and post-race lower-limb flush
  • Coordinated handoff with physiotherapy strength plans
Initial £65 · Follow-up £55

First Visit Preparation

            

              

                
                

                  Wear loose, comfortable athletic clothing so the injured joint or spinal region can be assessed clearly. Bring recent imaging reports or consultant letters if available, plus your insurer pre-authorisation code when claiming.
                

              


              

                
                

                  Self-pay patients can book directly across all four pathways without a GP referral. If using Bupa, AXA, Vitality, or Aviva, check whether your policy requires a triage call or referral code prior to your first visit.
                

              


              

                
                

                  When your insurer processes the first invoice against your annual policy excess, that excess amount is payable directly to the clinic. Subsequent covered sessions inside your authorised cap are billed to your insurer.
