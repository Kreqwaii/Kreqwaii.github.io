How closely does Pacific Dental align with the Alberta Fee Guide
Our treatment estimates follow current Alberta Dental Association Fee Guide benchmarks so employer insurance plans reimburse predictably with minimal unexpected gaps. Prior to starting any restorative or Invisalign care, you receive a written CAD treatment plan.
Do you submit insurance claims directly for Calgary patients
Yes. With electronic assignment, we submit directly to major Canadian carriers including Alberta Blue Cross, Sun Life, Canada Life, Manulife, and Green Shield so you only settle your remaining co-pay at the front desk.
Are Invisalign consultations and payment plans available
Yes. Using a digital iTero scanner, we show a projected alignment outcome before aligners are fabricated. After applying any orthodontic plan benefit, remaining balances can be spread across 12 or 18 monthly clinic installments.
How quickly can I be seen for a dental emergency in SW Calgary
We reserve daily triage windows for urgent toothaches, broken teeth, and dislodged restorations. Call (403) 686-2001 early in the day or submit an Urgent request through the booking form above.
