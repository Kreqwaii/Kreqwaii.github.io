Clinical Capabilities

Comprehensive medical, surgical, and diagnostic care

Every consultation includes a nose-to-tail physical examination, weight and body condition scoring, and clear written fee estimates prior to treatment.

From AUD $125

Pet Health Checks & Vaccinations

Tailored immunisation protocols for Sydney dogs and cats, alongside senior wellness screening and puppy or kitten developmental checks.

  • Canine C3, C5, and Canine Cough boosters
  • Feline F3 core vaccination and FeLV assessment
  • Cardiorespiratory, lymph node, and joint checks
From AUD $390

Veterinary Dentistry & Surgery

Ultrasonic dental scaling, polishing, and surgical extractions performed under monitored gaseous anaesthesia with intravenous fluid support.

  • Ultrasonic scale, polish, and periodontal charting
  • Desexing (spey and castration) with pain relief
  • Soft-tissue lump removals and wound repair
From AUD $260

Radiography & Ultrasonography

In-house diagnostic imaging for rapid assessment of orthopaedic injuries, thoracic conditions, abdominal organs, and pregnancy confirmation.

  • Low-dose digital radiography with rapid review
  • Abdominal ultrasound and bladder screening
  • Pre-surgical and geriatric diagnostic workups
From AUD $65

Microchipping & Parasite Control

NSW Companion Animals Register microchipping and year-round protection calibrated for Sydney paralysis ticks, heartworm, and fleas.

  • ISO-compliant microchipping and NSW registration
  • Annual ProHeart SR-12 heartworm injections
  • Paralysis tick and intestinal worming plans
