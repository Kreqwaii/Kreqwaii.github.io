Workflow Standard

How every bench job and custom part moves from intake to return

STEP 01

Compatibility & Intake

Send part numbers, photos, or dimensional specs via sales@lutzauto.com to confirm bench fixture support and obtain intake instructions.

STEP 02

Bench Diagnostics or Metrology

Electronic cores undergo pinout power-up, waveform capture, and thermal check. Mechanical parts are measured to verify critical mounting datums.

STEP 03

Precision Repair or Fabrication

Failed semiconductors, dried capacitors, and corroded traces are restored using high-temp rated components, or custom CAD parts are produced to spec.

STEP 04

Load Verification & Return

Every module is tested under simulated load cycles before sealing and ESD-safe return shipment from San Luis Obispo, CA.
