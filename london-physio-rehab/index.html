One-on-One Physiotherapy

Dedicated 60-minute initial assessments and 45-minute follow-ups for back and neck pain, sciatica, rotator cuff tendinopathy, sports sprains, and repetitive strain injuries. Combines hands-on manual therapy, IMS / dry needling, and progressive strength programming.

Post-Surgical & Joint Replacement Rehab

Structured orthopaedic protocols following total knee replacement (TKA), total hip replacement (THA), ACL reconstruction, rotator cuff repair, or fracture fixation. Carefully paced swelling control, joint range restoration, and safe return to stairs and walking.

Do I need a doctor referral first?

Physiotherapists in Ontario are primary-access regulated clinicians, so you do not need a referral to book an evaluation. A small number of extended health insurers request a physician prescription solely for reimbursement, which we help verify before billing.

How accurate is the estimator?

Session counts, recovery weeks, and CAD balances reflect typical Southwestern Ontario clinical ranges and fee schedules. Your physiotherapist confirms an individualized plan and exact treatment block during your 60-minute orthopaedic examination.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring photo ID, your extended health card or WSIB / MVA claim reference, any surgical discharge notes or X-ray / MRI summaries, and loose athletic clothing that allows comfortable assessment of the injured joint or spine.
