    Amsterdam Expat & Resident Guide

    How Dutch dental care works at Tandarts Jordaan

    New to Amsterdam or switching practices. Our English-speaking team guides you through registration, NZa invoices, and Netpoint reimbursement with full clarity.

    01 / INTAKE

    No GP Referral Needed

    Unlike specialist hospital care, you do not need a referral from your Dutch GP (huisarts). Simply register online with no waiting list and book your first intake at Westerstraat 176.

    02 / NZa RATES

    Government-Regulated Fees

    Every procedure code (such as C002 or M03) has a maximum national NZa rate. Tandarts Jordaan charges these standard rates all week with zero evening or Sunday surcharges.

    03 / NETPOINT

    Direct Claims & Card Pay

    Billing partner Netpoint sends itemized invoices to Dutch insurers first when covered. Visiting Amsterdam or on international insurance. Pay by PIN/card and receive a coded English/Dutch receipt immediately.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Insurance, evening hours & emergencies

    Do you charge extra for evening or Sunday appointments. +

    No. Unlike practices that add an evening or weekend surcharge (NZa code C021), Tandarts Jordaan applies the standard official NZa rates Monday through Wednesday until 21:45 and on Sundays from 09:30 to 18:15.

    Does Dutch basic health insurance (Basisverzekering) cover adults. +

    For adults 18+, Basisverzekering only covers rare specialist oral surgery. Routine check-ups (C002), cleaning (M03), X-rays (X10), and fillings (V91-V93) require supplementary dental insurance (Aanvullende Tandartsverzekering) or direct payment. Children under 18 are covered under Basisverzekering.

    How does the written cost estimate (begroting) work. +

    Before any treatment costing EUR 250 or more, you receive an itemized written NZa estimate showing every code and lab fee. You can verify reimbursement with your insurer before deciding to proceed. Smaller estimates are always provided on request.

    What if I have an acute dental emergency outside opening hours. +

    During clinic hours (including Mon-Wed until 21:45 and Sundays), call +31 20 612 12 43 first so we can reserve a same-day slot, even if you are not yet registered. Outside opening hours, contact Tandarts Bemiddelingsbureau Amsterdam on 020 303 45 00.
