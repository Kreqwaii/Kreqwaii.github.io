    Interim vs Full Servicing Inspection Scope

    Every service at CM Automotive uses manufacturer-approved components, preserves warranty validity, and concludes with a plain-English explanation of findings.

    ANNUAL / HIGH-MILEAGE

    Full Service Checklist

    2.5 - 3.2 hrs base
    • Complete vehicle health report for full peace of mind
    • Engine oil and oil filter replacement to OEM spec
    • Air and fuel filter replacement for cleaner performance
    • Brake system inspection, suspension and steering check
    • Tyre pressure and tread condition check
    • Battery condition test, lights, wipers and service light reset
    6-MONTH / INTERMEDIATE

    Interim Service Checklist

    1.5 - 2.0 hrs base
    • Engine oil and oil filter replacement between full services
    • Brake pad, disc and tyre safety check for daily driving
    • Top-up of vital fluids including coolant and screen wash
    • Visual inspection of key mechanical components to spot issues early
    • Exterior lights and wiper blade inspection
    • Service indicator reset upon completion
