What to Expect & Referrals

            
    
              
  • No GP referral is required for self-funding patients; book directly online or by telephone.
    • 
              
  • Insured patients should confirm pre-authorisation, GP/consultant referral rules, and policy excess before attending.
    • 
              
  • Bring relevant MRI, X-ray reports, medication lists, and comfortable shorts or a vest if preferred.
    • 
            

          

          

            
Chaperones, Children & Cancellations

            
    
              
  • Chaperones are always welcome. Patients under 16 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian throughout.
    • 
              
  • Young children may attend with you if childcare is unavailable.
    • 
              
  • 24 hours notice is required for changes or cancellations; cards are securely held via Stripe for late cancellations.
    •
