How & What We Treat

Clinical Physical Therapy Built Around Your Movement

PARC does not run cookie-cutter protocols. Every patient starts with root-cause movement investigation and receives hands-on care paired with active progressive rehabilitation.

Core DPT Care

Orthopedic Physical Therapy

Research-driven assessment of gait, joint mechanics, and neuromuscular control for back, neck, shoulder, hip, knee, and ankle conditions.

Pre & Post-Op

Post-Surgical Rehabilitation

Milestone-guided recovery following ACL, MCL, or meniscus repairs, rotator cuff tears, spinal procedures, and knee or hip replacements without reliance on opioids.

Return to Play

Sports Injury Rehabilitation

Targeted care for sprains, muscle strains, shin splints, runner knee, and throwing mechanics to move athletes from the sidelines back to full speed.

Hands-On

Manual Therapy & Joint Mobilization

Precision joint mobilization, myofascial release, IASTM soft-tissue work, and spinal manipulation to restore range of motion and address root restrictions.

Neuromuscular

Dry Needling Therapy

Modern musculoskeletal insertion of sterile filaments into tight myofascial bands to elicit a reset twitch response, reduce tension, and speed pain relief.

AIB-VRC Certified

Vestibular, Spine & Balance Care

Specialized vestibular rehabilitation for BPPV, vertigo, dizziness, migraines, and post-concussion balance adaptation alongside cervical and lumbar spine care.

Florida Direct Access (No Referral Required)

You do not need to wait weeks for a physician referral before starting physical therapy at PARC. Under Florida law, our licensed Doctors of Physical Therapy can evaluate and treat you immediately, saving additional doctor copays.

  • Treat up to 30 days under Direct Access (physician sign-off required beyond 30 days).
  • Medicare patients require a physician prescription prior to starting covered therapy visits.
  • Courtesy pre-visit benefit verification for copays, deductibles, and coinsurance.
