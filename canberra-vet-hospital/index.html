          ACT & Inner North Guidance
          
    Seasonal & Local Care Notes for Canberra Owners
    
        
    

        
    
          
    
            
    Grass Seeds & Spring Reserves
    
            
    Lyneham & O'Connor Trails
    
            
    
              Warm dry conditions around Canberra reserves increase barley grass seed injuries in paws, ears, and eyelids from late spring through summer. Check between toes after bush walks and book early if your dog develops sudden head shaking or licking.
            
    
          
    

          
    
            
    South Coast Travel
    
            
    Paralysis Tick Prevention
    
            
    
              While paralysis ticks are uncommon inside suburban Canberra, pets travelling east toward Batemans Bay, Ulladulla, or Broulee face high year-round tick risk. Maintain continuous vet-prescribed tick prevention before any coastal trip.

    Microchipping & Desexing
    
            
    
              Under ACT legislation, dogs and cats require microchipping and desexing within prescribed ages unless exempt. Our team assists with microchip verification, registration details, and age-appropriate surgical timing.
            
    
          
    
        
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
    
      
    
        
    
          Before You Book
          
    Frequently Asked Questions
    
        
    

        
    
          
    
            
              How accurate is the online AUD cost estimator?
              
            
            
    
              The estimator provides realistic itemised Australian Dollar ranges (inclusive of GST) reflecting standard consultation fees, vaccination protocols, weight-adjusted anaesthetic volumes, and common optional screening. Because surgical complexity, dental extractions, or unexpected findings can only be confirmed during examination, your veterinarian reviews a tailored written quote with you prior to proceeding.
            
    
          
    

          
    
            
              Does my pet need to fast before a dental or surgical appointment?
              
            
            
    
              For adult dogs and cats booked for general anaesthesia (dental care, desexing, soft-tissue surgery, or sedated imaging), food is typically withheld after 10:00 pm the evening before admission while fresh water remains available until morning. Puppies, kittens, and diabetic pets follow modified fasting instructions provided by our nursing team when confirming your booking.
            
    
          
    

          
    
            
              What should I bring to a vaccination or new-pet consultation?
              
            
            
    
              Please bring any previous vaccination certificates, clinical history from prior clinics, current medication packaging, and your microchip paperwork if recently adopted. Dogs should arrive on a secure lead and cats in a sturdy, closed carrier.
            
    
          
    

          
    
            
              How should I reach the clinic if my pet requires urgent attention?
              
            
            
    
              For urgent illness, trauma, breathing difficulty, or suspected toxin ingestion during clinic hours, call our reception desk directly on +61262413333 rather than submitting an email form so our veterinary nurses can triage your pet immediately.
            
    
          
    

          
    
            
              Can I send my itemised estimate directly to the clinic team?
              
            
            
    
              Yes. Both the Copy Summary button and the vets@canberravet.com.au email button automatically include your pet's species, age band, weight band, selected procedure, optional add-ons, itemised AUD range, and preferred booking window.
