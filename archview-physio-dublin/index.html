Health Insurance & Med 1 Tax Relief

All major Irish insurers recognise CORU physiotherapists, and many policies also reimburse CORU physical therapy and ANMT/IMTA neuromuscular massage. Where you qualify for alternative therapy cover, your clinician coordinates treatments to stretch your annual policy limits, and you can claim 20% Med 1 income tax relief on unreimbursed physiotherapy fees.

24-Hour Cancellation Notice

Because patients in acute pain rely on daily waiting lists, please give 24 hours notice to reschedule or cancel. Changes within 6 hours incur a €40 late fee, and unnotified no-shows are billed the full session fee (genuine emergencies excluded).
