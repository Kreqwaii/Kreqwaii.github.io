Adelaide Residential Safety Checklist

Isolate Mains Water Locate your SA Water meter box near the front boundary and turn the stop-cock clockwise until resistance stops.
STEP 02
Isolate Hot Water Tank Close the cold-water duo valve entering the base of the tank and switch off the dedicated breaker or gas supply.

Shut Off Gas Meter If you detect a gas odour, rotate the yellow-handled valve perpendicular to the pipe and open exterior doors.
