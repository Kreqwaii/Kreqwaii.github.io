Core Roofing Disciplines

Complete Pitched & Flat Roof Works

Every project in Southampton and neighbouring Hampshire districts is specified around UK BS 5534 wind-uplift standards, ventilated eaves detailing, and durable leadwork.

01 / FULL SCOPE New & Replacement Roofs

Full Roof Installation

Complete roof strip, rafter and felt inspection, breathable membrane, graded battens, new tiles or natural slates, dry-fix ridge and hip systems, and Code 4 milled lead flashings.

  • Structural batten and eaves tray upgrade
  • Mechanically fixed dry ridge and verge units
  • New Code 4 lead chimney aprons and valleys
  • Scaffold coordination and site waste clearance
02 / MAINTENANCE Responsive Callouts

Roof Repairs & Storm Maintenance

Targeted rectification for slipped or cracked tiles, perished mortar ridges, leaking lead valleys, and coastal Southampton wind damage before damp reaches internal ceilings.

03 / PITCHED Clay & Natural Slate

Slate & Clay Tile Works

Matched plain clay tiles, terracotta pantiles, and natural Welsh or Spanish slate re-laying tailored to Southampton Victorian, Edwardian, and 1930s housing stock.

04 / LOW PITCH EPDM & GRP Systems

Flat Roofs

Seamless single-ply EPDM rubber and GRP fibreglass decks for extensions, bay windows, dormers, and garages, including OSB3 deck replacement and drip-edge trims.
