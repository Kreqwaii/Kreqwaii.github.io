Westchase Roofing & Florida Insurance Questions

How does the 15-year roof age rule affect Tampa Bay homeowners insurance?

Under Florida carrier underwriting guidelines, roofs at or past 15 years old frequently require either full replacement or a clean 4-Point inspection verifying at least 5 years of remaining useful life. Pairing a full tear-off with a new Florida Building Code roof and OIR-B1-1802 Wind Mitigation report helps satisfy insurer underwriting and capture wind-loss mitigation discounts.

Why does Trust Roofing require $0 deposit on residential roofs under $30,000?

Florida homeowners face real risk from storm chasers demanding large upfront checks. Trust Roofing eliminates upfront risk on qualified residential projects under $30,000: we complete permitting, delivery, installation, and final walkthrough before collecting payment.
