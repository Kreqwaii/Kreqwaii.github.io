TRANSPARENT FINANCIAL PLANNING

Direct Billing, CDCP & Flexible Financing

We submit pre-determinations before treatment and explain co-payments, deductibles, and annual maximums in plain language.

01 / DIRECT BILLING

All Major Dental Insurers

Bring your benefits card and photo ID. We electronically bill employer plans, union coverage, and UBC/SFU student plans directly whenever your insurer allows, minimizing upfront out-of-pocket costs.

  • Electronic claim submission & written estimates
  • Pre-determination checks for crowns, implants, and aligners
  • Clear breakdown of co-insurance and annual limits
02 / FEDERAL COVERAGE

Canada Dental Care Plan (CDCP)

Align Dental Care proudly welcomes eligible Vancouver residents covered under the Canada Dental Care Plan (CDCP). Our administrative team helps coordinate coverage and any applicable co-insurance tiers.

  • Support with CDCP eligibility and pre-authorization documents
  • Coordination with secondary employer benefits where applicable
  • Clear explanation of CDCP fee schedule differences before care

iFinance Canada (Dentalcard)

Prefer predictable monthly installments for Invisalign, implants, or restorative treatment plans? iFinance Canada financing applies directly after your insurance benefits reduce the total balance.

  • Fast online application with flexible term lengths

  • Start needed care without delaying larger treatment plans
