TECHNICAL REFERENCE

Frequently Asked Roofing & Warranty Questions

How do I know whether my roof needs repair or full replacement?

It depends on the roof age, extent of granule loss or active leaks, and whether issues are isolated or widespread across decking and flashing. We inspect the roof, photograph what we find, and explain honestly whether targeted repair or full replacement is the sound investment.

How long does an architectural asphalt-shingle roof last in Middle Tennessee?

Architectural asphalt roofs typically last 20 to 30 years in Middle Tennessee, depending on balanced intake and exhaust attic ventilation, installation specification, and the selected manufacturer system. Standing seam metal systems commonly achieve 40 to 70 years.

What warranties cover a new roof installed by Nashville Roofing Company?

Qualifying replacements include Nashville Roofing Company's separate Lifetime Workmanship Warranty in addition to manufacturer-backed options such as the GAF Golden Pledge Limited Warranty for residential roofs or the GAF Diamond Pledge NDL Roof Guarantee up to 25 years for commercial systems.

Do you inspect hail or wind storm damage and assist with insurance documentation?

Yes. We provide emergency tarping when needed, document hail strikes per square and creased wind shingles with photos, and can meet your insurance adjuster on the roof during the inspection. Final coverage decisions are always made by your insurance carrier.

Can commercial roofing work be scheduled around operating business hours?

Yes. For retail centers, offices, warehouses, multi-family properties, and restaurants, we stage materials carefully and phase low-slope TPO, EPDM, PVC, or coating installations around tenants and operating windows to minimize disruption.
