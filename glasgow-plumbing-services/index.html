FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Booking, Payment & Turnaround Standards

How quickly can an engineer attend my property?

Most planned installation and maintenance work is booked a few days in advance, although we regularly squeeze urgent jobs in on the same day. For active leaks or burst pipes, our 24-hour emergency plumber service operates every day. Call 0141 295 0280 to confirm live van availability.

How and when do I pay for the work?

Payment is only due once the job is completed to your satisfaction. We accept cash, debit card, and bank transfers, with full itemised receipts provided upon settlement.

Which areas and property types do you cover?

We cover all of Glasgow (G1-G84) and surrounding Central Scotland districts. Our Gas Safe registered team handles both domestic households and 24-hour commercial plumbing contracts, from dripping taps to commercial boiler installations.
